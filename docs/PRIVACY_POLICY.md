My Clinic is a tool for **licensed doctors and approved lab/radiology staff** to
keep patient records, request investigations, and consult colleagues. It is
operated by **[your name or clinic/company name]**, [address], email

## 1. Who this applies to
- **Doctors and staff** who create an account in the app.
- **Patients** whose records doctors keep in the app. The patient's doctor is
  responsible for the record; we provide the system they use.

## 2. What we collect
| Data | Why | Kept |
|---|---|---|
| Account: name, email, phone, specialty, grade, hospital, licence or staff number, profile photo, photo of licence/ID card | To verify that every user is a licensed professional, and to show colleagues who they are dealing with | While the account exists, then [period] |
| Patient records entered by doctors: personal details, history, examination, investigations, surgery, photos and PDFs | To provide care; this is health data and treated as sensitive | As required for medical records under [Egyptian law / your policy, e.g. at least N years] |
| Consults and referrals: who shared what, with whom, when, and the patient's consent date | To let doctors share **only** what the patient agreed to, for a limited time | Like the record |
| Access log: who opened, changed, shared or exported a record, and when (no clinical content) | Security, and to answer patients' and regulators' questions | [period, e.g. 10 years] |
| Phone push address (if notifications are allowed) | To deliver alerts such as "New consult request". **Alerts never contain patient details.** | Until sign-out or uninstall |

We do **not** use advertising, analytics or tracking tools, and we do not sell data.

## 3. How data is protected
- Data travels only over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and is stored
  encrypted on servers in the **European Union (Frankfurt, Germany)**,
  operated by our hosting provider Supabase.
- On the phone, the offline copy of records and any photos waiting to upload
  are encrypted; the app locks with fingerprint, face or phone PIN, blocks
  screenshots of patient screens, and is excluded from phone backups.
- Every user only sees what the server allows: a doctor sees their own
  patients; a colleague sees only the parts shared with them for the time
  chosen; lab staff see only requests sent to their department, and only
  until the result is reviewed.

## 4. Sharing
Patient data is shared only:
- with the colleague or department the **patient's doctor** chooses, after
  confirming the patient's consent;
- with our processors, who only act on our instructions: **Supabase** (hosting,
  EU) and **Google Firebase Cloud Messaging** (delivering alerts, which contain
  no patient data);
- when the law requires it.

A doctor can export a record as a PDF to give to the patient or for their
care; once exported, the file is under that doctor's responsibility.

A doctor can also export a de-identified spreadsheet of their own patients
for approved research or a service review. Names, ID and file numbers, phone
numbers, addresses and dates of birth are removed before the file is made,
and each export is recorded in the patient's access log.

## 6. Your rights
Patients can ask their doctor, or us at [contact email], to see, correct or
obtain a copy of their data, to know who accessed it, or to withdraw consent
for future sharing. Medical records may have to be kept for a minimum period
by law even if deletion is requested. Users can delete their account by
writing to [contact email]. You can also complain to the Egyptian Personal
Data Protection Center (PDPC).

## 7. Children
The app is for professionals only and is not directed to children. Records
of patients who are children are entered by their doctor.

## 8. Changes
We will publish any change on this page and in the app.
